Scott Hougham.

Senior housing developer, investor and advisor. Head of Strategic Partnerships at Sage.

X LinkedIn

Scott started in senior housing at Atria Senior Living, now the second largest operator in the US. He worked in business optimization, tech product management, asset management and owner relations, and ended leading Atria’s development and redevelopment platform for the West Coast. He was the developer of Atria Newport Beach.

In early 2021 he spun out of Atria and founded Sophos Equities, a senior housing investment and advisory platform targeting underperforming assets. Sophos is Greek for wise or skilled. He chose it because the industry “has destroyed, ad nauseum, most other words for older/wiser people.” He advised Mesa West Capital, the lender, on the refinance of Thrive at Montvale.

He founded Reckon, a senior housing risk index. He is now Head of Strategic Partnerships at Sage. Thesis interest: owning aging in the US.

“Planes and Zoom make geography small.”

He lives in Auburn, California, in the Sierra foothills, with his wife and four kids, and splits time with Washington, DC. Smokes good cigars.

BA, Biology and History, University of Kentucky. MBA, University of Louisville. Master of Real Estate Development, USC.

Senior housing is an operating business that happens to be financed and valued as real estate. Scott Hougham
